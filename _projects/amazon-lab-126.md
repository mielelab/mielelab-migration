<p>Amazon Lab 126 – in 2019, Dr. Miele joined Amazon Lab 126 as a Principal Accessibility Researcher, helping to create delightful accessibility features for devices such as Fire TV, Fire Tablet, and Echo Show.  In 2024, he became a part-time Amazon Design Scholar, a role allowing him to continue to help guide the non-visual customer experience for Amazon devices, and advise widely across the company on inclusive design and accessible research. </p>
