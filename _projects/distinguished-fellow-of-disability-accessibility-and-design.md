<p>Dr. Miele is Distinguished Fellow of Disability, Accessibility, and Design at the  University of California at Berkeley’s Othering and Belonging Institute. This position fosters opportunities for close collaboration with faculty, students, and staff across the Berkeley campus working in the overlapping areas of disability, accessibility, and design. These include Berkeley’s RAD Lab, Jacobs Institute, Disability Cultural Center, and Masters of Design Program.</p>
