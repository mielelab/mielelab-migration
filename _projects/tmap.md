<p>Dr. Miele has a deep passion for, and commitment to, the importance of high-quality tactile maps and graphics in the lives and work of blind people.  Tactile Maps Automated Production (TMAP) began life as Dr. Miele’s 2003 postdoc project at Smith-Kettlewell. Now owned and operated by the Media and Accessible Design Lab (MADLab) at the San Francisco LightHouse, TMAP is a web-based service automating the process of creating well-formatted, tactile street maps of almost any location in the world, expanding options and opportunities for blind travelers as they live, work,and play.</p>
